How to Apply

Download &
Submit Your Application

Download our official fillable PDF application, complete all sections, and email your completed application along with all required documents to us. Incomplete applications will not be processed.

Required Documents — Submit with Application

Valid Texas Driver License (current, unexpired)
TXDPS-issued Security Officer Pocket Card (Level II or higher)
All security training certificates (Level II Classroom, Firearms Proficiency, etc.)
Any additional professional licenses or endorsements issued by TXDPS
