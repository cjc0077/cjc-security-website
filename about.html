Our Story

About CJC Security

Founded by a veteran law enforcement officer to bring real-world expertise, discipline, and integrity to private security — now serving clients statewide across Texas.

Who We Are

Experience You Can
Trust. Results You'll See.

CJC Security & Investigation LLC was established in 2023 in Edinburg, Texas, to fill a gap in the security market — a provider that operates with the discipline, training standards, and accountability of a law enforcement agency. What started in the Rio Grande Valley has grown into a statewide operation serving clients across Texas.

Our founder brings over 20 years of professional law enforcement experience, including a decade as a Hidalgo County Sheriff's deputy. That background shapes everything we do: how we hire, how we train, how we respond, and how we report.

We now serve businesses, schools, individuals, and organizations across South Texas, San Antonio, Abilene, El Paso, Lubbock, and the Dallas-Fort Worth area — delivering the same high standard of protection statewide.

"In law enforcement, you don't get second chances. We bring that same standard of performance to every security assignment we take."

— CJC Security & Investigation LLC, Founder
