What We Do

Comprehensive Security Solutions

From armed patrol officers to school safety audits, we provide the full spectrum of professional security services your organization needs.

Armed Security Guards

Highly trained, licensed armed security personnel for high-risk environments, event security, property protection, and deterrence of criminal activity.

Unarmed Security Officers

Professional, uniformed unarmed guards for retail locations, office buildings, residential communities, and access control operations.

School Safety Programs

Comprehensive school security including campus patrol, vulnerability audits, threat assessment, de-escalation training, and policy development for K-12 institutions.

Private Investigations

Discreet, professional investigative services including background checks, surveillance, missing persons, fraud investigation, and evidence gathering.

Counter-Surveillance Sweep

Professional detection and removal of hidden spy cameras, GPS tracking devices, and covert listening equipment in homes, vehicles, and offices.

Mobile Patrol Services

Routine and randomized mobile patrol for commercial properties, neighborhoods, and facilities requiring regular monitoring without a permanent guard post.

Executive Protection

Close-protection services for executives, dignitaries, and high-profile individuals. Threat assessment, advance planning, and discreet personal security details.

K9 Security Services

Trained detection and patrol K9 teams for narcotics, explosives, and intruder detection. Ideal for events, facilities, schools, and high-security environments.
